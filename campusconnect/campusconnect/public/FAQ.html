Frequently Asked Questions

Learn how to use CampusConnect to report and track issues around the university.

What is CampusConnect?

CampusConnect is a platform designed for students and staff to easily report, track, and prioritize campus issues like broken facilities, lost items, or general campus concerns.

How do I report a new issue?

Click on "Report issue" in the top navigation bar. Fill out a brief title, provide a detailed description of the problem, select the appropriate category (e.g., Classroom, Campus, Lost & Found), and click submit.

How does the upvote system work?

If you see an issue on the dashboard that also affects you, click the Upvote (▲) button! Issues with the most votes automatically move to the top of the dashboard so administration sees them first. Issues with 10 or more votes get highlighted.

What do the different issue statuses mean?
  • Open: The issue has been reported but not yet addressed.
  • In Progress: The administration or maintenance team is currently working on it.
  • Resolved: The issue has been successfully fixed or closed.
Can I delete or edit an issue I posted?

Currently, once an issue is posted, it becomes visible to the campus. If you made a severe mistake, please contact the administrators to have the ticket removed.
